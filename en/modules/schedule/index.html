Schedule / Documents

What you can do

Schedule / Documents brings together four things that usually end up scattered: appointments at a set time, birthdays of the people who matter to you (with a ready-made message to send), documents with an expiration date, like your passport and driver's license, and miscellaneous information — the kind you're always looking for and can never find. Each item can remind you before the date, as far in advance as you choose.

Where to start

Start with your documents: add the ones that expire and let the App remind you before they do — it's the kind of thing you only find out about at the worst possible moment. Then add your family's birthdays and your upcoming appointments.
