Bills to Pay/Receive

What you can do

Bills to Pay/Receive keeps your household bills in one place: what you have to pay and what you have to receive, each with its due date, amount, and, if you like, the person involved. The App reminds you before the due date, so no bill slips through the cracks, and shows what's still outstanding. A PDF report summarizes whatever period you choose.

Where to start

The first time, choose your currency. Then add the bills due this month — electricity, water, internet, credit card — with the due date and a reminder as far in advance as you prefer. The rest can be added little by little, as they come in.
