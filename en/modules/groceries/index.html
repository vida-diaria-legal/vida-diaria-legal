Supermarket

What you can do

Supermarket organizes your shopping in two steps. At home, you put together a pre-list of what you need; at the store, you open that pre-list with the ‘At the Store’ button and record the quantity and price of each item as you go. At the end, the App compares the calculated total with what you actually paid at the checkout. The price history of each product is saved, along with the supermarket where you bought it, for your next shopping trips — so you know where you shop for less. For products sold by weight, always record the price per kilo, not the package price: that's what keeps price comparisons accurate from one trip to the next. The list to take to the store and the purchase closing report can be generated as PDFs.

Where to start

Create your first pre-list with the ‘Create Pre-List (At Home)’ button and add the products you need — anything not yet registered is added on the spot, with its unit and department. When you go to the store, tap ‘At the Store’ and choose that pre-list. After your first completed purchase, your next pre-lists will already show the last prices you paid and estimate the total before you leave home.
