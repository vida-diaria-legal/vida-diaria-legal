Personal Vehicle

What you can do

Personal Vehicle brings together everything about your vehicle: fill-ups, maintenance, inspections, and mileage, with reminders before each scheduled date. Consumption Calculation uses the fill-ups you make day to day to show how much fuel your vehicle is really using, and it also works with electric and hybrid cars, including charging at home.

Where to start

Add your vehicle with its current mileage, the estimated amount of fuel in the tank, and the tank's total capacity — this information needs to be as accurate as possible, because it goes into the consumption calculation. Then log each fill-up: results appear starting from the first fill-up.
