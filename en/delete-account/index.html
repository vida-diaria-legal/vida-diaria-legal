Delete your account

You can delete your Vida Diária account, and all the data it contains, at any time.

Through the App (the fastest way)

  1. Open Vida Diária.
  2. Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the operation.

Deletion is automatic and immediate, and it cannot be undone.

Without the App installed

Send an email to privacidade@vidadiariaapp.com from the address registered to your account, with the subject «Delete account». We'll delete it and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

Your account and all the data you've entered, in every module. Any copies still in security backups are overwritten or anonymized within 30 days, as described in the Privacy Policy.
